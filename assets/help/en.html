If this is your first time, Getting started is enough. Open the rest when you need it.

Getting started
  1. In the Works & layout tab, pick a work and click its picture to upload an image.
  2. Enter the title, year, medium and the width and height in cm. The work hangs at that size.
  3. Drag the work in the gallery view to place it.
  4. Choose the look of the room in the Space & light tab.
  5. Press Save at the bottom. Visitors only see what has been saved.

Until you save, changes are visible only on your screen. Once saved, they appear at once for visitors who are viewing the exhibition.

Exhibition
  • Enter the title, dates, artist biography and artist's note. They are used on the entrance screen and the wall text.
  • Fill in the English and Japanese fields to show those texts when a visitor switches language. Empty fields fall back to Korean.
  • Copy exhibition link, Exhibition QR code: copy the address or save it as a QR image (PNG) for postcards and posters.
Adding and editing works
  • Use + Add work to create a work. Click a work in the list to open its editing box.
  • Click the picture, or drop a file on it, and the photo editor opens. To use the photo as it is, press Use this. Large photos are reduced automatically.
  • ✂ Edit photo: rotate by 90°, level in 0.1° steps (empty corners are cropped automatically), crop to a ratio, and adjust brightness. Brightness changes only how bright the photo is, not its colours. If you typed the work's size, the crop frame opens at the work ratio. To edit a photo already added, use the ✂ Edit photo button under it. Cancel or Esc changes nothing.
  • If an upload fails, a warning and an Upload again button appear on that work. If you save before it is uploaded, visitors still see the previous image.
  • Upload several photos: choose several photos and each one becomes a new work. The title comes from the file name and the height from the picture's proportions. Up to 20 at a time.
  • Edit several works at once: fill in the year and medium for all works, or only where the field is empty.
  • Change the viewing order with the order buttons. Delete needs two clicks.
Placing works
  • While the Works & layout tab is open, drag a work in the gallery to move it.
  • While dragging, the work clicks onto eye level (centre 1.5 m), the middle of the wall and the centres of neighbouring works, and a red line appears. Hold Shift to move freely.
  • In the position box you can change the wall or type exact numbers. There are also 5 cm nudge buttons, Centre on wall and Eye level.
  • Moving to another wall: choose another wall under Wall in the edit box and the work hangs at the right end of that wall; the works on the old wall and the new wall are each re-spaced evenly. In the gallery view you can also drag a work onto another wall, and it stays exactly where you drop it.
  • Space works on this wall evenly: only the works on that wall are spaced at equal gaps, in the same order. Other walls do not change. For a wall with a grid, adjust it in the grid settings. If the wall is too narrow and works may overlap, a warning appears.
  • The reset buttons return one work, or all works, to the automatic layout.
  • A warning appears when a work overlaps another.
Sets (diptychs, triptychs)
  • Press the link button on the right of the list to join a work to the one above it. Press again to release it.
  • Put the works next to each other in the order first.
  • A set moves together and has one caption. You can set a title for the set and the gap between works.
  • Release this set releases only that set; Release all sets releases every set. Released works stay where they hang.
  • Works in a grid cannot be joined as a set.
Grid layout
  • Open Arrange a wall as a grid, choose the wall, the rows, columns and gap, then pick a work for each cell.
  • The grid is centred on the wall automatically. Each column gets one light.
  • Captions can be per work, one for the whole grid, or none.
  • Dragging a work that is in the grid takes that work out of the grid.
Captions, frames, lighting
  • Caption position: right, left, below or above. A caption can also be hidden for a single work.
  • Frame: canvas, floating frame, frame, hanging scroll or antique. Set a default for the exhibition and override it per work.
  • The light intensity and shape can be changed per work.
Space & light
  • Choose the Room theme, floor, wall, ceiling and seating. You can also change the room's width, depth and ceiling height.
  • There are 15 themes. The 10 photo-material themes (Herringbone gallery, Limestone hall, Dark lounge and others) come with a matching wall, floor and ceiling; choosing a theme changes them as a set, and you can then change each one. The rail and spotlight colour (white or black) can be chosen too. The photo materials are textures from Poly Haven (CC0).
  • Room brightness and artwork lighting are adjusted separately. Captions brighten and dim with the lighting.
  • In the water garden, room brightness also sets the time of day: day, dusk or night.
  • Turn on the visitor shadows and choose the Shadow figures: people, people and cats, cats, or dinosaurs.
  • If editing feels slow, turn on Hide the floor reflection while editing at the bottom. It applies to this device only; visitors still see the reflection.
Music
  • Upload music file: MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV, up to 20 MB.
  • Set the track title, the volume and whether it plays automatically on entry.
  • Please upload only music you have the right to use.
Presets
  • Save the current layout and room size under a name, and bring it back later with Load.
  • Useful for trying out several arrangements. Presets are kept only after you press Save.
Links and QR codes
  • The buttons in the Exhibition tab lead to the whole exhibition.
  • Copy link to this work and QR code for this work lead straight to that work. They are handy next to the caption in a physical show.
  • For a newly added work, the link works after you save.
  • When printing a QR code, keep each side larger than 2 cm and do not crop the white margin.
Saving, undo, preview
  • Save: publishes your changes to visitors.
  • ↶ ↷: undo or redo one step at a time. Undo still works after saving.
  • Revert to last save: discards every unsaved change.
  • View as a visitor: closes the editing panel so you can see the real view. Press Edit at the top to return.
  • Export settings file: downloads the exhibition settings as a backup.
  • If you close the window without saving, the next time you open the editor on the same device it asks whether to Continue that work.
  • If another device saved first, you are told. Press Save once more to overwrite with your screen, or press Revert to last save to take the other version.
Auto tour
  • Open Works at the top and press Auto tour. The buttons disappear and the works are shown one after another.
  • Use it to record a video of the exhibition, or to run on a monitor in the gallery.
  • Click the screen or press any key to stop.
  • Add ?auto=1 to the end of the exhibition address to start the tour right after entering.

Moving several works at once

In the list on the Works tab, tick the small box at the top left of each picture. A dark bar appears above the list.

  • Align to eye level: puts the centre of each ticked work 1.5 m above the floor.
  • Align to the first work: uses the height of the topmost ticked work in the list.
  • Space evenly: with three or more ticked works on one wall, the two end works stay and the gaps between become equal.
  • Arrows, 5cm: moves the ticked works together by 5 cm.

A set moves as one piece, and works in a grid do not move. Use Ctrl + Z to undo.

Walking on a phone

On phones and tablets a round stick appears at the bottom right. Push it up or down to walk, left or right to turn. Dragging to look around and tapping the floor or a work still work as before.

Hiding and copying an exhibition

Turn on Hide from the exhibition list (Info tab) and save: the exhibition leaves the list and only people with the address or QR code can see it. Use it while preparing.

Copy this exhibition to make a new one is shown to admin accounts only. Enter a new address and a new, hidden exhibition is created with the same room, lighting and music. This exhibition does not change. When it is ready, turn hiding off in the new exhibition and save.

To let someone else edit the new exhibition, enter their ID as well. Their login account must be created separately under Authentication in Supabase.

Visits

Open Visits on the Info tab to see entries for today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days and in total. A visit is counted once when a visitor presses the enter button; people logged in to edit are not counted. Nothing about who visited is recorded.

Once, at the start, paste the part “7) 방문 기록” of supabase-setup.sql into the Supabase SQL Editor and run it. Until then no numbers appear, but the exhibition works as usual.

Guestbook

Visitors can leave their name and a short message (up to 300 characters) with the Guestbook button at the top. Messages are not shown at once: they appear to visitors after you press Publish in the Guestbook tab of the editor.

  • When messages are waiting, the editor tells you as it opens, and the tab shows a red number.
  • Published messages can be taken down at any time with Hide or Delete. Delete needs two presses.
  • Publish, Hide and Delete take effect at once, without Save.
  • For an exhibition that needs no guestbook, turn off Take guestbook messages for this exhibition in the Guestbook tab and save.
  • To stop prank posts, messages with web addresses (links), repeated messages, posting again within a minute and bursts of many messages are refused.
  • No e-mail or contact details are collected from visitors (only name, message and time).

Once, at the start, paste the part “8) 방명록” of supabase-setup.sql into the Supabase SQL Editor and run it. Until then the Guestbook button does not appear, but the exhibition works as usual.

Web catalogue

The Catalogue button at the top opens a catalogue that turns its pages like a book: two-page spreads on a computer, one page at a time on a phone. Turn pages with the arrows, the keyboard or a swipe; Save as PDF opens the print window, where you can save it as a PDF.

  • Automatic catalogue: made from the exhibition itself (cover, title page, artist note, one page per work, artist, colophon). In the editor’s Catalogue tab you can choose the cover work and write a foreword and colophon.
  • Catalogue pages: select the page images of a catalogue you have already made, all at once; they go in file-name order. You can also pick a print PDF as it is; each page is turned into an image. Reorder or delete pages in the same tab.
  • The first page (cover) is shown alone and the rest as spreads. Turn this off if your images are already spreads.
  • To stop visitors saving a PDF, turn off Let visitors save it as PDF. If you upload the original PDF too, visitors can download that original.
  • Copy catalogue link makes an address that opens the catalogue directly.

Uploaded pages and changed settings reach visitors after you press Save.

Backup & restore

The free server (Supabase) makes no automatic backups. Download one now and then under Backup & restore in the Exhibition info tab.

  • Download this exhibition's settings: saves this exhibition as address.json — the same as Export settings file in the bar below.
  • Load a settings file: brings this exhibition back to a saved file (or a full backup). It reaches visitors only after Save, and ↶ undoes it.
  • Download all exhibitions (admin): settings, guestbooks and visit counts of every exhibition in one file. Images and music are not included.
  • Check server storage (admin): how much of the free 1 GB the uploaded images and music use. Data sent to visitors is shown on the Supabase usage page.
  • A free server may pause after a week with no use. The GitHub repository contacts it every three days to prevent this; if it still pauses, restart the project in the Supabase dashboard. While it is paused, exhibitions open from the files in the exhibitions folder on GitHub. Those may be older, so after big changes upload the downloaded address.json there under the same name.
If something goes wrong
  • Visitors do not see my changes: check that you pressed Save.
  • The screen still shows the old version: reload the page. Cmd + Shift + R on a Mac, Ctrl + F5 on Windows.
  • An image will not upload: check your connection and press Upload again.
  • Editing is slow: turn on Hide the floor reflection while editing in the Space & light tab.
  • I changed something by mistake: press Ctrl + Z, or Revert to last save.
